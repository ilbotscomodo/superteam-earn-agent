## Single workflow: SOURCE > TEST > SCRIPT > RENDER > REVIEW > PUBLISH > MEASURE
1. **SOURCE (Investigator):** find original TikTok video and canonical URL; inspect audiovisual original when legally accessible; record creator, date, exact claim and timestamp. If inaccessible, mark `ORIGINAL_NOT_VIEWED`; do not target creator or imply certainty.
2. **TEST (Evidence auditor):** reproduce claim with free source data, official docs, read-only blockchain checks or code + test inputs/outputs. Classify separately: claim verified, refuted, unsupported, inconclusive. Disclose counterevidence and limits.
3. **SCRIPT (Editorial):** English 25–45s reaction: 0–2s hook, 2–8s claim, 8–28s visual proof, 28–40s measured verdict + "Show the receipts." No unsupported scam/fraud labels.
4. **RENDER (Producer):** 1080x1920 portrait master, fast male English narration, readable captions and visibly distinct avatar expressions. If male speech not available from local TTS, use a legitimately licensed alternative; do not silently use female voice. Check FPS/codec; validate clip plays without issue. Keep third-party footage contextual, minimal and lawful.
5. **REVIEW (QA):** match voiceover to original evidence; check maths, screenshot timestamps, rights, captions, loudness, subtitles and 9:16 mobile readability. Gate for targeted content = original accessible + contextual evidence.
6. **PUBLISH (Publisher):** TikTok via Metricool where tool supports it, recording scheduled ID and **verified** public URL after publication. Crosspost only polished masters to Instagram/YouTube. Never equate SCHEDULED/PENDING with PUBLISHED.
7. **MEASURE (Analytics):** record true per-post views, completion rate/average watch time where supported, shares, clicks, messages and leads. Don’t fabricate missing metrics.
8. **MONETIZE (Sales):** one specific qualified prospect/bounty per production cycle; personalize a small paid pilot, track email sent vs draft, scope, acceptance, invoice and **received money**.
